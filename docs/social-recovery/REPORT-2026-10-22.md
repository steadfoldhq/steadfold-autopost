## 8. Baseline findings (real data, `insights/` read at 16:39 UTC)

| Page | Followers | Reel median reach | Median avg watch | Feed median reach |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fully Funded | 6 | 45 | 3.8 s | 9 |
| Locked Hour | 3 | 24 | 4.2 s | 8.5 |
| Hourback | 4 | 18.5 | 4.1 s | 7 |
| Littlefold | 4 | 106.5 | 3.1 s | 6.5 |

What the data shows:

- Follows from feed posts: 0 on every page.
- Littlefold's ~1,600 Reel views are views, not people.

Why the pages aren't growing:

- Reels reach strangers but lose them in 3–4 s, so Instagram doesn't widen distribution.
- Feed posts reach only existing followers.
- There is no recurring reason to follow.
- Volume has come before quality.

Paid reach would not fix this yet. Full diagnosis: `docs/optimization-register.md`.
