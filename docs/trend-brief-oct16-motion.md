# Trend brief: edits, transitions and audio (Oct 16, 2026)

**For:** Steadfold motion and sound: Fully Funded, Locked Hour, Hourback, Littlefold.
**Bottom line:** in October the turn lands on the beat drop. Reveals, outfit swaps and before/afters all cut on the drum hit, with text arriving word by word on the beat. Our Reels were cutting on time, not on the music. All 8 style prototypes now land the "Try this" turn on each track's measured drop.

## Adopt now
1. **Turn on the drop** (the Ramalama mechanic). The "Try this" or fix beat lands on the track's measured drop, with a hit SFX and a camera kick. This is in all 8 prototypes, and drop times are in `tools/music-library.json`.
2. **Word-by-word text on the beat** ("Take that chance"). Each word springs in on its own. Hold times stay at or above the 0.4 s/word + 0.6 s reading rule.
3. **"Getting robbed" absorption POV for Locked Hour.** The world changes around someone glued to their phone (Styles 5 and 8). It is a ready-made hook for the page's core message.
4. **"No no no / yes yes yes" before/after** for Hourback (manual way vs AI way) and Fully Funded (spreadsheet chaos vs one move).
5. **Bass-drop cutting.** Whip pans and zoom punches go on low-end hits, with a slow-snap-slow ramp into the drop (Styles 2, 4, 6).
6. **Mask reveals**, in our own shapes: pink iris (S2), liquid flood (S4), page turn (S1), paper rip (S3).
7. **Loading-bar format** for Locked Hour: "focus loading 0→100%" over one hour of work.

## Set first (no one in our niches does these yet)
- **Locked Hour: "the lock clicks."** On the drop, Lockie's shackle snaps shut, with a shutter click plus a bass hit (Style 8). This is a sonic and visual logo for every Locked Hour Reel.
- **Object-drop desk.** Props fall on the beat with bounce, squash and contact shadows (Style 6). For Fully Funded, coins, jars and bills drop as the maths builds.
- **Notification explosion.** The chaos blasts off-screen on the drop, then a calm focus widget builds (Style 5). It fits Hourback ("AI clears the pile") and Locked Hour.
- **Scrapbook milestones for Littlefold.** Real-footage polaroids, tape and die-cut stickers, with the camera travelling across the page (Style 7).
- **One drop hit per page.** The same impact sound at each page's turn, so ears learn the brand.

## Skip
- Dance and lip-sync trends: our pages are faceless.
- Halloween and creepy-fact formats: off-theme, and gloomy is banned.
- Brainrot edits: they damage the brand.
- Trending licensed songs in auto-posts. Later notes that "original audio" is not royalty-free. We use our own ElevenLabs tracks, and platform library sounds only when posting by hand.
