No, this looks like a scam. Red flags:

1. Asks for your Instagram login: "enter your Instagram login so we can verify your stats". This is the biggest one. It's how accounts get hijacked, and no real brand needs your password (they can ask for a screenshot of your insights).
2. Asks you to pay first: "after you pay a $49 refundable onboarding fee". Real brands pay creators, not the other way around. "Refundable" is the hook.
3. Sketchy download: "download our creator app from this link". An unknown app or link can carry malware or a phishing page.
4. Pressure tactic: "Please reply within 24 hours as spots are very limited!" Scammers use urgency so you won't stop to check.
5. Pay too high for a cold offer: "we'd like to offer you $2,000 for one Reel". That's a lot for an unvetted pitch with no negotiation.
6. Generic flattery with no specifics: "We LOVE your content 😍". It doesn't mention any actual post, and it's the same message sent to many people.
7. Vague company claim: "a fast-growing skincare brand". There's no website, contact name, or brief.

What a real offer usually includes: a named contact on a company email domain, a clear brief (deliverables, usage rights, timeline), and a written contract or agreement.
Payment terms are stated (often net 30, or part up front), with free product or a deposit. They never ask for your password or any money from you.

What to do: don't click or download anything, don't reply with details, and report and block the sender. If you already entered your login, change your password and turn on two-factor authentication now.
