**Piece A: #1, "Nonna turns 90 tomorrow. In Italian?"**
It has a person, a milestone, a deadline and the problem, all in six words. #2 is vague because "her language" hides the hook. #3 is clear but flat, and "Grandma" is less vivid than "Nonna."

**Piece B: #2, "31 years. You get 2 minutes."**
This is the strongest scroll-stopper because the contrast creates tension. Its weakness is that it never says "speech" or "tomorrow," so the visual or the next line has to establish that it's a retirement speech. If you can't rely on that, #1 is the clearest fallback. #3 is clunky.

**Piece C: #3, "Slept through their 30th. Now what?"**
It's a specific, relatable kind of guilt, and it's clear. #1 works but is generic. #2 reads like an ad giving orders.
